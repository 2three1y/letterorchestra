How it works

I made this because I saw Orca, a music thing where every letter is an instrument, and I was like ok but what if typing a text message was a whole song. So now it is. You are welcome.

  • Letters play notes from a scale that basically can't sound bad. I tried. I typed my whole name and it slapped.
  • Vowels (a, e, i, o, u) get a soft pad sound. Consonants get the plucky sound, and the Instrument menu changes that one.
  • Space is a rest, so you get a little pause.
  • Numbers are drums: 1 is a kick, 2 a snare, 3 hats, and the rest are mixes. Spam 1212 for instant beat.
  • ! is a crash, ? is a rising boop, and . is a tiny click.
  • Play it back plays your whole text as a loop. Escape or Stop shuts it up.
  • Export WAV saves one pass of your text as a WAV file at your tempo and instrument, so you can drop it straight into a DAW. Yes, your group chat can now be a sample pack.

Nothing talks over your screen reader while you type. The sound is the feedback. No sound plays until you press or type something.
