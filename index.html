            How TabLab works

            Build beat uses the seed in the Seed box, so the same settings and the same seed always make the same beat. Build again picks a new seed and makes a new version. Change the seed yourself to get an old one back.

            Every track goes intro, drop, bridge, drop 2, big ending. The intro and bridge are muffled, the drops open up, and there is one beat of silence right before each drop.

            Melody from my letters reads Your text from the start again at every section. Letters go up a scale from a to z, like they do when you type them here, but in the beat's minor key. Lead sound picks what plays the melody: the vibe's own synth, any Letter Orchestra instrument, or each letter's own instrument from when you typed it.

            Bomb timer adds beeps and ticking that speed up into the drops and the ending. Pitch dives make the 808 and the vowel chops slide way down at the end of sections. Tape stop slows the whole beat to a stop at the end of the first drop and at the very end. Vinyl crackle adds record noise and makes the intro more lo-fi.

            The vowel chops are synths shaped like vowels, not real voices. Download beat WAV saves a 16 bit, 44.1 kHz stereo file you can drop into FL Studio. Escape stops playback.

            Guide

            I made this because I saw Orca, a music thing where every letter is an instrument, and I was like ok but what if typing a text message was a whole song. So now it is. Here's how to drive it.

            Turn the sound on

            Press Turn sound on at the top. You'll hear a little three note chime. If you hear nothing, flip the ring switch on the side of your phone off silent and turn the volume up. Phones are shy about web sounds until you tap something, so that button is the tap.

            What each key does

            • Letters play notes from a scale that basically can't sound bad. I tried. I typed my whole name and it slapped.
            • a is the lowest note and z is the highest.
            • Vowels (a, e, i, o, u) play a soft, long version of the instrument, like a pad. Consonants play the short, plucky version.
            • Numbers are drums: 1 kick, 2 snare, 3 closed hat, 4 open hat, 5 high tom, 6 low tom, 7 kick plus snare, 8 kick plus hat, 9 snare plus open hat, 0 kick plus open hat. Spam 1212 for instant beat.
            • Space is a rest, so you get a little pause.
            • ! is a crash, ? is a rising boop, and . is a tiny click.
            • Every new line is its own track, and all the lines play at the same time. Put drums on line one, bass on line two, melody on line three. Congrats, you have a band.

            Play it back

            • Play it back plays every line together, one character per eighth note.
            • Stop, or the Escape key, shuts it up.
            • Loop is on by default. Each line loops on its own, so a short line repeats under a long one. Turn Loop off to play it once.
            • Tempo goes from 60 to 200 BPM. You can edit the text while it plays and it picks up your changes live.

            Instruments

            Pick an instrument, then type. The letters remember the instrument they were typed with. Switch instruments mid-typing to stack sounds in one track. There are 17: Chiptune, Dreamy, Bass, Piano, 808 Bass, Pluck, Super Saw, Bell, Organ, Flute, Strings Pad, Steel Drum, Guitar Pluck, Choir Ooh, Wobble Bass, Keys and Reese bass. All made from scratch, no samples. Wobble Bass wobbles in time with your tempo, so it's dubstep ready. Drums and punctuation always sound the same.

            Effects

            The Effects section has seven effects, a bit like the mixer in FL Studio. Each one has a button to turn it on and a slider for how strong it is. They work on live typing, on playback, on the pattern and on the audio file. Effects are for the whole song, not single letters. Pro tip: Bitcrusher plus Reverb plus Filter sweep is instant lo-fi.

            Demos

            Each demo button loads a starter song in a genre, with its own tempo, instruments and effects. It replaces your text, so save first if you made a banger. Then press Play it back. Read the lines to see how it's built, then wreck it and make it yours.

            Song title, saving and audio

            • Type a name in Song title. Your files get that name, so a song called Tab downloads as Tab.wav and Tab.json. No title means Untitled song.
            • Save song downloads a song file (.json) with everything in it: letters, pattern, piano roll notes, mixer, effects and tempo. Open song loads one back.
            • It also saves by itself in this browser as you go, so closing the tab doesn't wipe your song. New song starts fresh (it asks first).
            • Download as audio (or Export WAV, same thing) makes a WAV file of your letters plus the pattern, with your effects and mixer settings, so you can drop it straight into a DAW. Yes, your group chat can now be a sample pack.
            • Play song plays the letters and the pattern together, in time. Stop song stops both.

            Step sequencer

            • Each track is one sound with 16 steps, four steps per beat. Every step is a button that says its track, its number and on or off, like "Kick, step 5, off". Double tap to flip it.
            • It starts with Kick, Snare, Closed hat, Bass and Lead. Add a track picks any drum or any of the 17 instruments. Each track also has Clear and Remove.
            • With a keyboard, arrow left and right move between steps, up and down jump to the same step on the next track.
            • Play pattern loops it. The tempo slider here is the same tempo as the Play section.

            Piano roll

            • Pick an instrument track, then add notes with any pitch from C2 to C6, any step, any length.
            • Notes is a list, each note reads like "E4, step 3, length 2". Double tap one to select it, then use Note up, Note down, Octave up, Octave down, Move earlier, Move later, Longer, Shorter, Hear note or Delete note.
            • Grid shows one octave at a time. Every square is a button like "C4, step 3, off". Double tap to add or remove a note.
            • Turning on an instrument track's step in the sequencer adds its default note there, and it shows up in the piano roll too.

            Mixer

            Every track, plus Letters (your typed lines), gets a volume slider, a Mute button and a Solo button. Solo one or more and only those play. Master volume turns the whole thing up or down, and it all goes into the audio file too.

            TabLab beat maker

            TabLab builds a whole beat for you. Pick a vibe or a preset, then press Build beat, then Play beat. Turn on Melody from my letters to hear what you typed as the melody over the beat, and pick a Lead sound to play it with any instrument from here.

            VoiceOver tips

            • Nothing talks over VoiceOver while you type. The sound is the feedback.
            • Use whatever typing style you like (standard, touch or direct touch). The sound plays as each character goes in.
            • If VoiceOver's voice makes the notes go quiet, turn Audio Ducking off in the VoiceOver Audio settings.
            • Jump around by headings: Song, Play, Step sequencer, Piano roll, Mixer, Guide, Effects, Demos and Try a phrase are all headings. Each sequencer track is a smaller heading too.
            • Studio changes (a step flipped, a note moved, a file saved) are read out once in a short message. Typing letters stays quiet.
            • The sliders go in steps of 5. Swipe up or down on them to change the value.
            • No sound ever plays until you press or type something.

            Effects

            Reverb

            Space. Makes everything sound like it's in a big room.

            Delay

            Echo echo echo. Timed to your tempo.

            Distortion

            Crunch. Turn it up for chaos.

            Filter sweep

            Muffles the highs and slowly sweeps, like a DJ knob.

            Chorus

            Makes one sound feel wide and wobbly, like two players.

            Bitcrusher

            Lo-fi. Chews the sound into old video game crumbs.

            Sidechain pump

            Ducks the volume on every beat. That house music breathing.
